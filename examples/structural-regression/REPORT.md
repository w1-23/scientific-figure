## What each case asserts

- **materials-layered** — a stacked coating with six pores: the repetition binding must still count five or more separate openings, so merging them into one band fails.
- **chemistry-scheme** — three rings and their bonds: deleting a ring and the bonds fails the bound objects instead of passing silently.
- **physics-optics** — laser, converging beam, lens and screen: drawing the beam straight through the lens fails the `converging` binding.
- **soft-illustration** — a gradient blob with a diffuse edge: the pack must hold no `strong_structural` class, and the blurry boundary stays `needs_review` (no edge evidence) rather than being forced into hard paths.

## Limits

- These are synthetic, small references. They verify the evidence wiring and detection, not visual quality or scientific correctness.
- The negative-space heuristic still reports `needs_review` on stacked layers; the materials case therefore binds repetition and boundary, not negative space.
- `constraints check` compares edges and coarse region signatures only; it does not verify colour gradients, labels or meaning.
